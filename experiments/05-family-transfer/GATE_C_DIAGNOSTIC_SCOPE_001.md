# Gate C diagnostic-only scope 001

Status: preparation only. Simulator implementation and execution unauthorized.
Confirmatory outcomes remain sealed. The universe is unchanged.

## Purpose and limits

Assess precision and feasibility under explicitly stated synthetic assumptions.
A favorable result leaves exact negative-sequence holdout overlap and unverified
historical family separation unresolved. It cannot authorize a confirmatory
claim. Insufficient resolvability is an expectation, not a required outcome.
No seed, grouping, universe, or scenario may be selected to obtain that outcome.

## Inherited rules

RESOLVABILITY_PLAN.md and applicable existing protocol documents remain binding.
Their exact committed bytes are captured in the companion inventory. This scope
changes no effect boundary, power target, coverage criterion, replicate count,
validation order, or failure classification. In particular, the +0.05 scientific
boundary is distinct from any power target evaluated at +0.10.

## Required specification before implementing or authorizing a simulator

1. Primary weighting estimand, plus clearly labelled secondary summaries.
2. Calibration/evaluation allocation and exact empirical 5% FPR threshold rule,
   including ties, interpolation and threshold transfer.
3. Paired score-generating model, baseline operating points, effect calibration,
   within-group dependence and between-arm dependence; all grids fixed upfront.
4. Operational groupings and overlap assumptions, without equating group counts
   to effective sample sizes or treating partitions as certified biological families.
5. Interval method, resampling unit, nested threshold refitting, and treatment of
   mixed-label groups and any dependence shared across calibration/evaluation.
6. Seeds, Monte Carlo budgets, known-answer fixtures and binding validation gates,
   with validation completed before any resolvability surface is inspected.
7. Per-scenario decision reporting. Any claim across scenarios must have its
   aggregation rule fixed before simulation; favorable scenarios cannot erase
   unfavorable scenarios or the standing admissibility limitations.
8. Audit and immutable archive schemas, execution gates and failure handling.

No numerical choices absent from the inherited documents are supplied here.
The next implementation must resolve those choices prospectively. Gate C outputs
must remain separate from biological performance results and confirmatory access.
