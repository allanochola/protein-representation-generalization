RECURRENCE HEADROOM
-------------------
The frozen sparse-probe instrument is capable of very high coordinate
recurrence. At least one cell reached 100/100 recurrence in S1, S3, S6,
and S7. S7 reached 100/100 maximum coordinate recurrence at target_n
100, 120, and 139.

Thus lack of coordinate recurrence is not by itself an explanation for
the G failure.

INTERPRETATION
--------------
Two distinct failures are now separated.

1. S7 generator/control failure

   The planted S7 shortcut coordinates have fixed fitted-sign orientation
   conditional on coordinate identity. The current mixed-orientation
   redundant-proxy construction did not generate recurring-coordinate
   sign instability.

   Merely lowering rho_shortcut does not introduce a mechanism that
   changes the orientation of a recurring planted coordinate.

2. G calibration-mechanism failure on the consumed suite

   The frozen signed Jaccard did distinguish 114 individual perturbation
   pairs from their unsigned counterparts, but those differences never
   altered either middle order statistic in any calibration cell.

   Consequently median aggregation made G_stat operationally
   non-discriminating from I_stat throughout block 3000-3099.

This does not establish that median signed Jaccard can never discriminate
a suitably constructed sign-instability control. Nor does it justify
selecting a replacement G statistic from this consumed block.

It establishes that the current S7 construction supplied no systematic
planted-coordinate sign ambiguity and that the signed distinctions which
did occur elsewhere were too sparse to affect the frozen G aggregation.

Any redesign must therefore consider separately:

    (a) a generator/control mechanism capable of systematic sign reversal
        on recurring coordinates; and

    (b) whether the frozen median G aggregation can detect the intended
        regime under the actual R2/L1 probe mechanics.

No gamma threshold was selected or altered by this analysis.
